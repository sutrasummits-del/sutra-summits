01 — The Organization
S

Where ideas meet influence.

Sutra Summits is a student-led debate and Model United Nations organization dedicated to creating meaningful platforms for young minds.

From competitive debate to diplomatic simulations, Sutra provides opportunities to think critically, communicate confidently and engage with ideas that matter.

We believe that strong leadership begins with the ability to listen, question, reason and speak with purpose.

04 — The MUN Room

Every role has
a purpose.

SUTRA MUN will bring together leadership, government, opposition and diplomatic roles to create a structured and engaging experience.

♛

Prime Minister

Lead the government, establish its direction and defend its central position.

◆

Deputy Prime Minister

Support the Prime Minister, strengthen the government's case and respond to opposition.

◈

Government

Build a coherent case, defend policy and persuade the room through strategy.

⚔

Opposition

Challenge the government's position and present a compelling alternative.

◎

Delegates

Represent assigned positions, negotiate, research and engage in diplomatic discussion.

⚖

Executive Board

Guide proceedings, evaluate performance and maintain the standard of the summit.
